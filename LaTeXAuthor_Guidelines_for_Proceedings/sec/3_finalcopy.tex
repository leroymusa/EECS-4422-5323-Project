The probabilistic GMM absorbs bright saliva pixels that the hard clusters sometimes treat as background, yielding near-perfect recall at \(K=3\). The addition of a fourth component, selected by the highest PLL over ten EM runs (some seeded by k-means centers, some random), regains precision and raises Dice while still delivering high recall. This likelihood-guided selection, reinforced by the probability maps and morphological cleanup, provides both the segmentation masks and the log-likelihood measurements that describe the fit quality of each run.

\section{Conclusion}

This report documents an unsupervised saliva segmentation workflow: Method A’s MATLAB thresholding, Method C’s k-means clustering, and Method D’s likelihood-aware Gaussian mixture model. Method D keeps recall high and, at \(K=4\), raises Dice while using pseudo-log-likelihood to select the most probable model, demonstrating that probabilistic modeling and multi-start EM produce stable masks in this safety-critical, low-data setting.
